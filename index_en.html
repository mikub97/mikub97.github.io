Research

I work on where mathematical concepts come from — and the same question comes back in lessons.

OЯTHO is a game in which two children steer a ball through a maze together: one controls the horizontal axis, the other the vertical. Nobody explains a coordinate system to them — the system shows up as a way of getting through to each other. We tested, across more than 1,200 players, which order of tasks actually builds that understanding. Abrahamson, D., Tancredi, S., Xiao, A., Li, X., Weiss, M., Potega vel Żabik, K., & Dimmel, J. K. (2026). It's Our Move: Mathematical Perception Emerges for Coordinating Joint Action. Canadian Journal of Experimental Psychology (in press). Student projects

Graph-based analysis of fragrance compositions

Malina W.

A dataset of roughly 60,000 perfumes. Data extracted and joined in SQL, prepared in pandas, then a graph: ingredients as nodes, co-occurrence as edges. Centrality measures and clustering showed which notes organise compositions.

PythonSQLpandasNetworkXPlotly

Delivery planning system

Zuzia D.

A Flask application: shops place orders, drivers deliver them in vans of limited capacity. Database schema written from scratch in SQL, queries with joins and no ORM, a map of the stops in Folium, the route computed with a nearest-neighbour heuristic. Plus architecture documentation.

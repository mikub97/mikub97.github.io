Why me

There are hundreds of maths tutors. The difference is that how people learn mathematics is my research subject, not just my job.

“It's Our Move: Mathematical Perception Emerges for Coordinating Joint Action” — a study of OЯTHO, a game in which two children steer a ball through a maze together: one controls the horizontal axis, the other the vertical. Nobody explains coordinate systems to them; the coordinate system appears as a way of coordinating with each other. We ran the study with over 1,200 players to find out which sequence of tasks actually builds that understanding. Abrahamson, D., Tancredi, S., Xiao, A., Li, X., Weiss, M., Potega vel Żabik, K., & Dimmel, J. K. (2026). Canadian Journal of Experimental Psychology. doi:10.1037/cep0000414

Projects I supervised

The best evidence that something worked is a thing that works. Two projects carried through to the end by people I taught — from an empty file to a repository worth showing.

Graph-based analysis of fragrance compositions

Malina W.

A dataset of roughly 60,000 perfumes. Data extracted and joined in SQL, prepared in pandas, then modelled as a graph with ingredients as nodes and co-occurrence as weighted edges. Centrality measures and clustering revealed which notes organise compositions.

PythonSQLpandasNetworkXPlotly

Private repository — happy to walk through it on a call.

Delivery planning system — a web application

Zuzia D.

A Flask application in which shops place orders and drivers deliver them in vehicles of limited capacity. Schema written from scratch in SQL, joined queries without an ORM, a Folium map of the stops, and route planning by nearest-neighbour heuristic — plus architecture documentation with layer and request-flow diagrams.
